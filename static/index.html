⚠ This reading is in the hypertensive crisis range

A reading higher than 180/120 can be dangerous. Wait 5 minutes, then measure again.

If it's still this high and you have any of these, call 911 (or your local emergency number) now:

  • Chest pain or shortness of breath
  • Back pain, numbness or weakness
  • A change in vision or trouble speaking

If it's still this high and you feel fine, contact your doctor right away.
